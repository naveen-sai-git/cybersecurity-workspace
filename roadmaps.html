01

Networking & Security Foundations

OSI model, TCP/IP, firewall concepts, IDS/IPS fundamentals, and security architecture basics. Build the vocabulary of defensive security.

02

Linux Administration & SIEM Basics

Navigate Linux environments, manage logs, and get hands-on with Splunk and ELK Stack. Understand how SIEMs aggregate and correlate events.

03

Threat Detection & Log Analysis

Write detection rules, analyze security logs, understand attack patterns, and distinguish between false positives and genuine threats in real alerts.

04

Incident Response & Playbooks

Develop and execute IR playbooks, perform forensic triage, coordinate containment, and lead post-incident analysis and reporting.

05

Threat Hunting & Intelligence

Go beyond reactive monitoring. Use threat intelligence feeds, hunt for adversary TTPs using the MITRE ATT&CK framework, and proactively find threats.
