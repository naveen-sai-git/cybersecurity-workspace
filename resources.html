02 / CERTIFICATIONS

Credentials that move careers.

The certifications hiring managers actually look for — from entry-level foundations to elite offensive security designations.

01
OSCP
Offensive Security Certified Professional — the gold standard for penetration testers. 24-hour practical exam in a live network environment.
Details
02
CompTIA Security+
The most widely held entry-level security certification. DoD-approved and vendor-neutral. Excellent first certification for career changers.
Details
03
eJPT (eLearnSecurity)
Junior penetration tester certification. Fully practical, browser-based exam. The most accessible first pentest cert — recommended before OSCP.
Details
04
PNPT (TCM Security)
Practical Network Penetration Testing. Affordable, realistic exam format with a 5-day engagement and professional report. Industry loved.
Details
05
CEH (EC-Council)
Certified Ethical Hacker. Widely recognized in corporate environments and government. Knowledge-based exam covering offensive tools and techniques.
